## Exam technique for this course
- The lecturer's slides are full of **worked "Exercise → Solution" pairs** (biceps torque, PCSA × 90 N/cm², pennate F cos α, √(gL) for an adult, a child and the Moon,
  jump height). Expect the same patterns with new numbers.
- Many questions are **"explain / compare / distinguish"**. Answer with a table or a bullet list: definition, mechanism, example.
- For calculations, write the **model and its assumptions first** (e.g. "constant volume", "mass lumped at the hip", "massless spring").
  Some homework questions are open to interpretation, so stated assumptions earn the marks.
- Watch the **inconsistencies in the slides** (flagged with ⚠️ in the study guide and HW solutions). If you use a formula, quote its form.
